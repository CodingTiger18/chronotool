PRIVACY POLICY

PRIVACY FIRST: OmniTime has no user accounts, requires no sign-ups, and asks for no personal profiles. Your selected cities, time format, and theme choices are stored only on your own device using your browser's local storage.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because OmniTime is a lightweight, client-side utility:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • We do not maintain user databases or profile tracking.
  • Your customized timezone schedules and pinned cities remain private on your computer or phone.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

OmniTime uses browser localStorage purely to remember your preferences between visits:

  • Pinned Locations: The cities and timezones you add to your matrix.
  • Display Preferences: Your selection of 12-hour or 24-hour time and Dark or Light theme.

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache or site data.

3. Automated Server Logs

Like almost all websites, the hosting server automatically records standard technical connection data. These logs may include:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Files and pages requested
  • Access dates and timestamps

This information is used strictly for server health, bandwidth monitoring, uptime diagnostics, and defending against cyberattacks or abuse.

4. Cookies & Third-Party Advertising

To support this free service, we display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense):

  • Third-party ad networks use cookies and web beacons to serve relevant ads based on prior visits to websites.
  • You can manage or opt out of personalized ads by visiting Google Ads Settings or through industry opt-out portals such as aboutads.info.
  • You can also configure your web browser to reject or delete cookies entirely.

5. Contact Us

For questions or feedback, please email support@ChronoTools.online.
