Global Clock: Timezones, Simplified & Untangled

OmniTime is an all-in-one world clock, converter, and scheduler. Compare multiple zones at a glance, plan international meetings, and track global market hours effortlessly.

Your own time zone is pinned on top and the current hour sits in the centre. Add cities with the search box, pick a date if daylight saving changes fall between you and a colleague, then look for columns where every row is green. Offsets come from your browser's time zone database, so daylight saving is handled for you. Share copies a link that restores your exact set of cities.
